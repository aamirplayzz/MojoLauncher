# Anime Launcher Privacy Policy

1. Anime Launcher does not collect, store or send any personal data to its developers. There are no analytics, ads or trackers in the launcher.
2. The app uses the internet only to download game files, libraries, mods and other content you ask for (for example from Mojang, Modrinth, CurseForge and Maven repositories), and to sign in to your account.
3. When you sign in with a Microsoft or Ely.by account, the sign-in is handled by that provider. Your login tokens are stored only on your device.
4. Game files, worlds, logs and crash reports are stored on your device. They are shared only if you choose to share them (for example with the "Share log file" button).
5. This policy may be updated. Questions and issues: https://github.com/aamirplayzz/MojoLauncher/issues

Anime Launcher is a modified version of MojoLauncher and is not an official Minecraft product. Not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
